A crisp, satisfying salad with creamy Caesar dressing, pan-seared chicken, and crunchy croutons. Ready in 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 large head romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1/2 cup Caesar dressing
- 1/2 cup croutons
- 1/4 cup shaved Parmesan
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- 1 lemon (optional, for squeezing)

## Instructions

1. **Cook the chicken.** Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and a drizzle of olive oil. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken 6–7 minutes per side until cooked through and golden. Let rest 3 minutes, then slice.
2. **Prepare the lettuce.** Wash and dry the romaine. Chop into bite-sized pieces and place in a large bowl.
3. **Dress the salad.** Add Caesar dressing and toss to coat evenly.
4. **Assemble.** Top with sliced chicken, croutons, and shaved Parmesan. Squeeze lemon over the top if desired.
5. **Serve immediately** — croutons lose their crunch if left to sit.

## Variations

- **Grilled** — grill the chicken instead of pan-searing for a smoky flavour
- **Vegetarian** — omit the chicken and add chickpeas for protein
- **Anchovy** — stir 1–2 anchovy fillets into the dressing for depth
