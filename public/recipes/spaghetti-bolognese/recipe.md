A rich, slow-simmered meat sauce served over al dente spaghetti. This is the kind of dish that gets better the longer it cooks.

## Ingredients

- 400 g spaghetti
- 500 g ground beef
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 stalks celery, finely diced
- 400 g canned crushed tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 120 ml dry red wine
- Salt, pepper, and olive oil

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the vegetables.** Heat olive oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrot, and celery. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 8–10 minutes until softened.
2. **Brown the meat.** Add garlic and ground beef. Break the meat up with a spoon and cook until browned, about 5–7 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
3. **Deglaze.** Pour in the red wine and let it bubble for 2 minutes, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
4. **Add tomatoes.** Stir in the crushed tomatoes and tomato paste. Mix well.
5. **Simmer.** Reduce heat to low, cover partially, and simmer for at least 30 minutes — 45 minutes is better. Stir occasionally.
6. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente.
7. **Combine and serve.** Toss the spaghetti with the sauce or serve sauce on top. Finish with grated Parmesan.

## Notes

- ~~Using pre-minced garlic from a jar~~ — use fresh garlic for best results.
- The sauce freezes well for up to 3 months.

```
Sauce thickness guide:
  Too thin → simmer uncovered for 10 more min
  Too thick → add a splash of pasta water
```
