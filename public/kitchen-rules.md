# Kitchen Rules

The following are the rules that you should follow while using all shared kitchens:
-  Wash hands, use gloves, and maintain sanitized food handling areas. Use ventilation fans when cooking.
- Clean all tools, countertops, and appliances immediately after use. Wash, dry, and return items to their proper places.
- Label all items in the fridge / freezer with a name and date. Unlabeled or expired food should be discarded.
- Respect communal equipment and notify staff of any broken items.
